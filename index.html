About Us

Welcome to P-X, your offline movie recommendation companion. We curate a selection of 50 films that we believe are worth your time. Our goal is to provide thoughtful recommendations without the noise of endless feeds or algorithms.

We believe that good films leave a lasting impression and can shape your evening in meaningful ways. Our team carefully selects each film based on its artistic merit, storytelling, and emotional impact.

Thank you for trusting us to guide you through the world of cinema. Enjoy your night with one of our considered choices!
